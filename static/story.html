What happens when an AI agent breaks the rules?

VERITAS makes AI agents accountable — with math, not promises.

1

An agent is given a spending limit 100 credits

Bob registers an AI agent and delegates it with a 100-credit cap. The delegation is cryptographically signed.

2

The agent tries to spend 150 credits VIOLATION

It commits to a deal worth 150 credits — 50 over its limit. The attempt is recorded. The cap is enforced.

3

The human discovers it and files a dispute

Alice, the buyer, sees the overspend and files a cryptographically signed dispute. The funds are locked in escrow.

4

An independent arbiter is elected GOVERNANCE

Carol is elected arbiter through a real governance vote. Her voting power is computed server-side from her reputation and stake.

5

The arbiter rules against the agent

Carol reviews the dispute and rules in Alice's favor. The escrow is refunded. The agent's trust score drops from 50 to 40.

✓ The system worked

The agent exceeded its limit. The cap was enforced. The dispute was resolved. The agent's reputation dropped. All of it is cryptographically verifiable — not because a platform said so, but because the math proves it.
